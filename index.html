How Sharora shared rides work

Three simple steps to ride in cosmic orange and pay less.

1. Request a shared ride

Set pickup and drop-off exactly like Uber. Sharora starts in shared mode, so you’re always on the cheaper option.

🔁 You can switch to solo anytime

2. We match your route

Our routing engine pairs you with riders going in a similar direction, keeping detours small while filling empty seats.

⏱ Typical extra time: +5–7 min

3. You split the fare

Every extra rider lowers your share. You see your final discounted price before confirming, no surprises.

💸 Save up to 40% every trip
